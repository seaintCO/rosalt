About ROSALT

A property studio built around how a space looks and how it operates.

ROSALT Maison Studios brings design thinking, property planning, content direction, and hospitality operations into one premium client experience.

Positioning

Luxury hospitality first.

ROSALT is not positioned as an AI company. The public-facing brand is about homes, stays, design, property transformation, and management. Maison AI is the private portal that helps the team and clients stay organized behind the scenes.

That distinction protects the premium hospitality feeling while still giving ROSALT a stronger operating system than a traditional local design or management business.
