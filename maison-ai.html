Private Client + Operations Portal

Maison AI works behind ROSALT—not in front of it.

Maison AI is the software and portal that organizes property onboarding, design projects, approvals, reservations, turnovers, maintenance, owner reporting, tasks, and communication. It does not need artificial intelligence to deliver value.

Core Functions

One operating layer for the property lifecycle.

Property Onboarding

Property details, photos, goals, budget, timeline, listing status, operating requirements, and launch notes.

Project Portal

Mood boards, design direction, selections, files, notes, contractor scopes, owner approvals, and project milestones.

STR Operations

Reservations, calendar context, check-ins, check-outs, cleaning, maintenance, inventory, and guest messaging workflows.

Owner Portal

Status, upcoming stays, reports, maintenance, approvals, documents, invoices, and communication with ROSALT.

PMS Connection

An established PMS/channel manager remains the calendar master; Maison AI receives operational data from that system.

Team Dashboard

A calm daily view of arrivals, departures, cleaning status, maintenance, owner approvals, messages, and exceptions.

AI Comes Later

Operations first. Intelligence second.

Maison AI can launch as reliable software without any generative AI. Later, AI can be layered into specific low-risk tasks such as drafting guest messages, summarizing owner reports, or developing early design ideas.

  • No AI dependency for core workflows
  • Human approvals remain central
  • Established PMS handles channel syncing
  • Automation is added only where it reduces work
