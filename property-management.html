Property Management

Boutique hospitality management for short-term rentals.

ROSALT operates as the co-host and manager. The property owner keeps ownership of Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com, payout accounts, tax details, and listings while ROSALT coordinates the day-to-day hospitality operation.

Operating Model

Owner-controlled accounts. ROSALT-managed operations.

The model is intentionally clean: the owner remains the account and payout owner; ROSALT receives operational access as co-host/manager and is paid by management percentage or a monthly management fee.

  • Listing and reservation oversight
  • Arrival and departure calendar
  • Cleaning assignment and turnover status
  • Maintenance and vendor coordination
  • Inventory and guest-readiness checks
  • Owner approvals, updates, and documentation
  • Guest communication workflows
Channel Structure

Airbnb · Vrbo · Booking.com

At launch, these channels connect through an established PMS/channel manager. That PMS is the calendar master that prevents double bookings. Maison AI receives the operational data rather than trying to replace mature channel infrastructure on day one.
